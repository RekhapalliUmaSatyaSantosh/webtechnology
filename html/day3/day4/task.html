Shri Subhash Chandra Bose

Subhas Chandra Bose was one of the greatest freedom fighters of India. He was popularly known as “Netaji” and inspired millions of Indians to fight for independence. He founded the Indian National Army (INA) to fight against British rule. His famous slogan was “Give me blood, and I will give you freedom.” He strongly believed in courage, discipline, and patriotism. Subhas Chandra Bose remains a symbol of bravery and dedication to the nation.


Shri Bhagat Singh

Bhagat Singh was a brave Indian freedom fighter who played an important role in the struggle against British rule. He was born on September 28, 1907, in Punjab and became inspired by patriotic movements at a young age. Bhagat Singh believed that sacrifice and courage were necessary to achieve freedom for India. He is well known for his revolutionary activities and his slogan “Inquilab Zindabad,” which means “Long Live the Revolution.” At the age of 23, he was executed by the British on March 23, 1931, along with his companions Rajguru and Sukhdev. Bhagat Singh is remembered as a national hero whose patriotism and bravery continue to inspire millions of Indians.


Shri Chandra Shekar Azad

Chandra Shekhar Azad was a brave Indian freedom fighter who played an important role in India’s struggle for independence. He was born on 23 July 1906 in Bhavra, Madhya Pradesh. Azad joined revolutionary activities at a young age and fought against British rule with great courage. He was associated with the Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA) and worked with revolutionaries like Bhagat Singh. He believed that freedom was the right of every Indian and inspired many young people. Chandra Shekhar Azad sacrificed his life for the nation on 27 February 1931 in Allahabad.
